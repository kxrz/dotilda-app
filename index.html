The ritual · under two minutes

One question, at the hour you choose. Then nothing.

Your sky · 2026

Miss a day? Nothing breaks.

The whole year fits on one screen: twelve rows, one dot per day. An empty day is a tiny grey dot, a breath in the grid. No streak, no red, no guilt.

  • Yours aloneNo account. Everything stays on the device. Export or move your journal any time.
  • Made for e‑inkPure black on white, large type, no scrolling, no animation. Page with the volume keys.
  • Rewards looking backMonthly and seasonal reviews, letters to future you, and your year as an EPUB book.
  • Quiet by designOne reminder a day, at your hour. No nudges, no rankings, no social feed.
  • Breathe firstspira, a one‑minute breathing break, plus a timer and stopwatch the Kompakt lacks.
  • Free, for goodNo purchase, no subscription, no ads. Open source under Apache 2.0.
