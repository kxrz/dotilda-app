# Testing Dotilda

Thank you for trying Dotilda before everyone else. Here is how to install it, what to look at,
and how to tell us what you find.

## Install

1. Download `dotilda.apk` from the [latest release](https://github.com/kxrz/dotilda-app/releases/latest).
2. Install it:
   - **Mudita Kompakt:** with Mudita Center (Manage apps › install from file), or with
     [Obtainium](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium) pointed at `https://github.com/kxrz/dotilda-app`.
   - **Other Android 11+ devices:** open the APK and allow the install.
3. From then on, Dotilda tells you herself on Today when a new version is ready
   ("Psst. I have a new version for you.") and installs it from Settings.

## What to look at

Use Dotilda every day, the way you would anyway: one question, a short answer. Along the way:

- **Every day:** does the reminder come at your time? How does it show on your launcher?
- **Once:** answer a photo question (does your camera app open? is the picture readable in four
  greys?), and start a journey.
- **Once:** Settings, second page: export your journal, then import it (on another device if you
  have one); export the year as a book and open it on your e-reader (KOReader or another).
- **On e-ink:** anything that leaves ghosts on the screen, text too small or too tight, a page
  that should turn but doesn't.

Your journal never leaves your phone: Dotilda has no account and sends nothing. The only network
access is the update check, which you can turn off in Settings.

## Tell us

- **Something wrong:** [open an issue](https://github.com/kxrz/dotilda-app/issues/new?template=bug.yml).
  If Dotilda crashed, Settings › Diagnostics › "Export the crash report" saves a text file to attach.
- **An idea:** [share it](https://github.com/kxrz/dotilda-app/issues/new?template=idea.yml).

Please don't paste anything from your journal unless you want it public.
